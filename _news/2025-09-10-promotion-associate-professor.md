I am deeply grateful to share that I have been promoted to Associate Professor with tenure in the Department of Computer Science at UC Santa Barbara. This milestone would not have been possible without the exceptional contributions of my students — past and present — whose creativity, perseverance, and dedication have been the driving force behind our research. I am equally thankful to my collaborators across academia, industry, and government, and to the many mentors and colleagues who have offered guidance and support throughout this journey. I look forward to continuing our work on self-driving networks and data-driven broadband policymaking with renewed energy.
