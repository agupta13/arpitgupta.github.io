---
title: "Reflections from the ESnet Workshop on AI for Networking"
date: 2025-09-20
---

I had the privilege of participating in a [workshop organized by ESnet](https://lnkd.in/g_dgxTyV) (the Department of Energy's high-performance networking facility) on the role of AI in networking. The discussions reinforced my belief that we are at a genuine inflection point: the convergence of foundation models, programmable infrastructure, and growing operational complexity is creating unprecedented opportunities for AI-driven network management. The workshop brought together researchers from national labs, universities, and industry to chart a path forward for deploying AI in production research networks. It was a highly productive exchange that will shape our thinking on self-driving networks in the coming years.
