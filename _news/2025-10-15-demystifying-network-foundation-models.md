---
title: "Paper on Demystifying Network Foundation Models Accepted at NeurIPS D&B 2025"
date: 2025-10-15
---

Our paper, "Demystifying Network Foundation Models," has been accepted at the **NeurIPS 2025 Datasets and Benchmarks** track. This work introduces an intrinsic evaluation framework for systematically analyzing the learned representations of network foundation models, moving beyond task-specific benchmarks to understand what these models actually learn about network traffic structure. The framework provides tools for probing, visualizing, and comparing the internal representations across different model architectures and pre-training strategies. We believe this kind of rigorous representation analysis is essential for building trustworthy and generalizable foundation models for networking. The preprint and code are publicly available.
