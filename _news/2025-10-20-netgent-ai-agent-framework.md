---
title: "Introducing NetGent: AI-Agent Framework for Network Workflow Generation"
date: 2025-10-20
---

We introduced [NetGent](https://lnkd.in/gxaUjHnQ), a new AI-agent framework for programmable application workflow generation in networking. NetGent enables researchers and operators to automatically generate and customize over 50 application workflows for network data collection, testing, and evaluation, significantly reducing the manual effort typically required. This work addresses a critical gap in the ML-for-networking pipeline: the ability to systematically produce diverse, realistic workloads for training and benchmarking. NetGent has been accepted at the **MLSys workshop at NeurIPS 2025**. This is an exciting step toward making self-driving network research more accessible and reproducible.
