I participated in the [6GIC-CLICK event on Telecom AI Solutions and Future Research Challenges](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/6gic-click-featuring-telecom-ai-solutions-and-future-research-challenges-tickets-1850635002109), which brought together researchers and industry practitioners working at the frontier of AI for telecommunications. The event featured discussions on how foundation models and AI agents can transform network operations, planning, and security in next-generation networks. It was a valuable opportunity to exchange ideas with the international telecom research community on the practical challenges and opportunities of deploying AI in production network environments.
