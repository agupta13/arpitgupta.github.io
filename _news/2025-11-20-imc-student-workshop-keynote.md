I delivered the keynote at the ACM Internet Measurement Conference (IMC) 2025 Student Workshop, titled "Making the Net Work for All: From Measurements to Impact." The talk explored the intersection of broadband policy and AIOps, highlighting how rigorous network measurements can inform policy decisions and how AI-driven operations can improve network performance for underserved communities. I also discussed our recent work on the Virginia JCOTS broadband affordability and adoption study as a case study of how measurement-driven research can directly shape legislative deliberations. It was inspiring to engage with the next generation of measurement researchers on these critical topics.
