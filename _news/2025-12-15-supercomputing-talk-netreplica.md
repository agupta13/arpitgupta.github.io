I had the privilege of visiting St. Louis and presenting our recent work at the Supercomputing conference. The [talk](https://lnkd.in/g5Sk-Wvi) covered our efforts on NetReplica, NetGent, and the Intrinsic Evaluation Framework — a suite of tools that together form a programmable data-generation substrate for networking research. The core idea is to enable researchers and practitioners to systematically generate high-fidelity network data for training and evaluating ML models, addressing one of the most persistent bottlenecks in applying machine learning to production network operations. It was a fantastic opportunity to engage with the HPC and networking communities on these shared challenges.
