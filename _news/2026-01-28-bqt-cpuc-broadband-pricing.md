---
title: "BQT Data Informs California Public Utilities Commission Report on Broadband Pricing"
date: 2026-01-28
---

Our Broadband-Plan Querying Tool (BQT) is making a tangible policy impact in California. The [Public Advocates Office at the California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC)](https://lnkd.in/gwJg8SDd) released a report that leverages BQT's address-level pricing data to analyze broadband competition and affordability across the state. A key finding is that gigabit-level competition drives significantly lower prices, with differences ranging from $15 to $40 per month depending on the market. At scale, these price differences translate to over $1 billion in potential statewide savings for California consumers. Kudos to Owen Park for his outstanding contributions to this effort. This is a strong validation of the data-driven policymaking approach we have been championing through BQT.
